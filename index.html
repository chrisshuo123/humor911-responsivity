About Us

Serious about funny

HUMOR911 is a comedy agency and production house built on one belief: laughter is essential infrastructure. We represent comedic talent, develop original shows, and produce live experiences that people talk about on the drive home.

Our mission is simple — find funny people, give them great stages, and treat every audience like they showed up for something special. Because they did.

  • Bold Voices

    We champion comedians with a point of view, not a formula.

  • Craft First

    Great comedy looks effortless. We obsess over the effort.

  • Every Room Matters

    From open mics to main stages, every audience gets our best.

The badge means business. Funny business.
